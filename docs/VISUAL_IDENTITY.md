# MOBRA visual identity

MOBRA uses an original shield, mobile-laboratory, and traceability motif. The artwork is vector-first and was created for this repository; it does not reproduce or imply endorsement by WHO, ISO, CDC, NIH, military, government, biohazard, or other third-party marks.

## Assets

- `assets/branding/mobra_logo_main.svg` / `.png`: full lockup for light backgrounds.
- `assets/branding/mobra_logo_horizontal.svg` / `.png`: compact header lockup.
- `assets/branding/mobra_icon.svg` / `.png`: icon-only use.
- `assets/branding/mobra_favicon.png`: small browser icon.
- `assets/branding/mobra_monochrome.svg`: single-colour fallback.
- `assets/branding/mobra_brand_guidelines.pdf`: printable usage guide.
- `assets/branding/brand_palette.json`: exact HEX values and contrast notes.

## Palette and usage

Deep navy communicates governance and trust; teal communicates laboratory systems; cyan communicates technology and traceability; amber marks conditional attention; red is reserved for critical warnings; green indicates an acceptable status; slate and mist support readable content. Status labels are always shown in text, so colour is never the only signal.

Use the full logo on clear white or mist backgrounds, keep the icon legible at small sizes, preserve the aspect ratio, and retain whitespace around the mark. Do not stretch, add third-party logos, recolour arbitrarily, or present MOBRA as certified, endorsed, or institutionally validated.

The app, reports, printable forms, posters, resource catalogue, manuscript card, backup README, and email body use the same central metadata and palette.
